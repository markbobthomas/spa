Made with care.

Stillwater is a native Godot spa-building sandbox with a shared, stylized art direction. Its baths and building modules are authored in Blender to sit beside KayKit’s furniture, cafe props, scenery and animated characters.

The engine

Godot 4.6.3 is distributed under the MIT license. The original engine license and third-party copyright notices accompany this build.

The art

Publicly downloadable game art by Kay Lousberg / KayKit. Each included pack is CC0 1.0, permitting redistribution and modification.

  • Furniture Bits — couches, armchairs, chairs, shelves, tables, lamps, cabinets, rugs and plants. License.
  • Restaurant Bits — cafe counters, dining tables, ceramics and food props. License.
  • City Builder Bits — garden benches and bushes. License.
  • Medieval Hexagon Pack — garden trees and scenery. License.
  • Character Pack Adventures — spa guests use the Rogue’s original model, rig and animations. Equipment and cape references are removed; clothing materials are adapted to the spa palette. License.

Original spa fixtures, architecture, plant additions and materials are created for Stillwater. Download the editable Godot project for the scene code, asset sources, hashes, licenses and Blender authoring script.
